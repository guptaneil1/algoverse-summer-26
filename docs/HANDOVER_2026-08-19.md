# Handover — 2026-08-19

Written after the primary pilot executed and was analysed. Supersedes
`HANDOVER_2026-08-18.md`, which predates any chain and whose "Nothing blocks it" was false
in six separate ways.

## One-line state

The apparatus works and the grid ran. **No allocation claim survives** — the primary
contrast failed on one budget axis and every non-trivial secondary contrast failed on the
other. What the run delivers is a variance estimate, a feasibility measurement, one clean
null, and a design fix. **Do not re-run yet:** F-021a shows a re-run would reproduce the
second failure.

## What happened

25 chains, 5 policies × 5 frozen seeds, horizon 10, on 4× RTX 4090. Longest shard 6.75 h,
57.9 min per chain, roughly $20 at an observed $3/hour. All completed, none failed.

**Certification: 10 `invalid`, 15 `valid_with_limitation`.** The five `joint` chains fail
on both budget axes; the five `selection_only` chains fail on total optimizer tokens.

Full record: `docs/runs/primary_pilot_2026-08-18_results.md`.

## The two budget failures

`PROTOCOL.md` §4 names two conditions — matched lifetime human-origin tokens **and**
matched total optimizer tokens. Both failed, for unrelated reasons, and only the first was
ever checked.

**Human axis (F-020).** `joint` consumed 674,193 at every seed against the other spending
arms' ~749,850 — a 10.1% shortfall. Terminal reconciliation raised its spending cap but
left its feasibility floor at 1 token, so its urgency rule decided the spend. Fixed and
pinned by test.

**Total axis (F-021).** Realised totals span **2.26%** across arms, above the 2%
threshold. This was never asserted anywhere — not by `--check-only`, not by the guard, not
by any statement made about the run. The guard now checks both axes.

## What survives

| | |
|---|---|
| **Between-chain variance** | CVs 0.41–1.08% against a 2% threshold. Measured *within* arms, so unaffected by either failure. Releases `COMPUTE.md`'s compute gate. Sizing: `docs/decisions/powered_design_sizing_2026-08-19.md` |
| **One clean contrast** | `schedule_only` vs `random` is matched on both axes (human −0.00%, total +0.02%). It does not differ detectably: **when the budget is spent does not change the outcome** at this operating point. A null, and the only comparison this run supports |
| **Feasibility** | 57.9 min/chain, 6.75 h, ~$20, measured |
| **The apparatus** | Runs the full recursive loop on real models and emits certifiable artifacts |

**Withdrawn:** the `selection_only` advantage. It is real in the data (8.37% vs random)
but arrives with 1.7% more total training tokens in the same direction. Roughly five times
the confound, so suggestive — but not budget-matched, and not reported as a result.

## Seven defects, all found after a passing dry run

`FAILURE_LOG.md` F-016 → F-021a.

| ID | What |
|---|---|
| F-016 | Launcher's budget guard could never pass — no config with a control arm could satisfy exact equality |
| F-017 | Corpus paths resolved against the upstream checkout; all 25 chains dead in seconds |
| F-018 | Two more copies of F-016's rule in the certification path |
| F-019 / F-019a | Partition provenance in a vocabulary the manifest builder did not read; then the fix verified against the config on disk rather than the one the launcher builds |
| F-020 / F-020a | Terminal reconciliation raised `joint`'s cap but not its floor |
| F-021 / F-021a | The total-token axis was never checked; 10 chains are invalid; a re-run would not fix it |

**Three share a shape:** a check whose intent was documented and whose implementation did
not achieve it, with no test asserting the intent. A guard never observed to bind is a
guard whose binding is unverified.

**Two limits on the dry run, now written down.** It prints the upstream command rather
than executing it, so nothing depending on the subprocess environment is covered. And
score-dependent policies allocate from simulated statistics, so **budget matching for them
is not testable by simulation at all** — `random` and `schedule_only` reproduced their
dry-run spends to the token; `joint` did not.

`docs/RUNBOOK_PILOT_LAUNCH.md` §5a now requires one real chain plus `validate_run.py`
before any grid. That step found five of the seven.

## Decisions

**P-001 – P-011 are accepted by the project owner**, not ratified by the team. No
independent review took place; three sit in other members' CODEOWNERS areas. `DECISIONS.md`
states exactly what that means and why leaving them pending was worse.

**All six U-items are closed.** U-006 settled at 2% after checking it against measured
anchors (`docs/decisions/effect_threshold_review_2026-08-19.md`); it cannot now be changed
honestly, because primary outcomes are open. U-004b closed as unreachable.

**P-011 is the design decision the run forced.** Rescued human examples now *displace*
synthetic records rather than being appended, so the total-token condition holds by
construction. It also fixes a confound nobody had noticed: under addition the no-rescue
control trains on less data than every spending arm, so "spending helps" could never be
separated from "more data helps". **Unvalidated — no chain has run under it.**

## Things a fresh session should know

- Result numbers in the manuscript come from `paper/tables/pilot_macros.tex`, generated by
  `scripts/generate_pilot_outputs.py`. Prose cites macros; a bare decimal in a section is a
  test failure, deliberately.
- Artifacts are in `results/runs/primary_pilot_2026-08-18/`. Chain results are tracked; the
  162 MB of manifests are not, but `ARTIFACT_HASHES.json` makes an archived copy
  verifiable. The archive is a ~36 MB tarball held outside the repository — it is the only
  copy and is unreproducible without another $20.
- **Heredocs here eat backslash escapes.** Use Write/Edit for anything containing them.
  This bit three times in one session: a shim's `\n`, a LaTeX regex, and a `\begin{itemize}`
  that became `egin{itemize}`.
- `git add -A` has swept in unwanted directories three times. Stage explicitly.
- `results/runs/**` is gitignored except chain results, summaries, aggregate, hash ledger
  and READMEs. The rule needs `**`, not `*`.
- The pod bills while idle. The last run finished around 04:25 and was noticed at 05:24.
