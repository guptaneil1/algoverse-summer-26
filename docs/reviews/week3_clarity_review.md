A clarity review requires a technically literate person who is **not on this
team** to read the materials without verbal help. It cannot be simulated, and a
teammate explaining the paper to another teammate is not a clarity review.

### Honesty rule

A harsh review is useful evidence. Record the strongest criticism verbatim, the
response, the exact wording changed, and every unresolved concern. Do not
summarise a negative review as approval, and do not paraphrase criticism into
something milder.
