\section{Conclusion}

This project defines an evidence-gated test of how to spend a finite stock of human-origin training exposure in a recursive human--synthetic chain. Its distinctive object is not human mixing, data selection, or dynamic scheduling in isolation. It is the matched comparison among fresh-random, schedule-only, selection-only, and joint time-and-mode policies under exact lifetime human-token and total optimizer-token accounting. The protocol treats one complete chain as the experimental unit, evaluates fit on untouched human text, separately audits tail retention, and includes an incomplete-monitor intervention because targeted allocation can optimize the wrong reference. Recent work on dynamic mixtures, iterative weakness-guided curation, and biased local-reference selection narrows the novelty claim and strengthens the required baselines rather than validating the proposed policy \cite{zhao2026regmixd,wang2026tikmix,luo2026kite,qiao2026selectionbias}.

\textbf{RESULT\_PENDING:} no novel treatment outcome is currently supported. If the joint policy clears the preregistered effect threshold against the strongest eligible non-joint baseline with valid budgets and uncertainty, the conclusion will be limited to improved allocation in the frozen domain, model, horizon, and budget. If the paired contrast falls inside the practically equivalent region, the conclusion will favor the simpler baseline and reject added adaptivity for this setting. If the joint policy is worse, destabilizes training, or damages omitted modes, the conclusion will report harm and treat monitoring dependence as a failure boundary. If intervals span beneficial, equivalent, and harmful regions, the study will be labeled inconclusive rather than null.

Any leakage, budget mismatch, positive-control failure, artifact-integrity failure, or post-hoc exclusion invalidates the corresponding efficacy claim. A failed novelty review requires withdrawal or reframing even if experimental outcomes are favorable. Conversely, a valid negative result remains informative because it tests whether coupling timing and targeting adds value once training exposure is controlled. General claims about preventing model collapse, preserving all rare modes, optimal allocation, or frontier-scale systems require broader domains, models, horizons, baselines, and independent replication. The final wording will be selected from the preregistered outcome-contingent alternatives and linked to immutable artifacts; it will not be chosen for rhetorical impact.
