## Defects found and recorded

Fifteen, in `FAILURE_LOG.md` as F-006 through F-015. The ones that would have produced
wrong numbers rather than errors:

- **F-010b** — the manifest counted whitespace words, not tokens. Budget matching would have
  been off by up to 20% invisibly, because both sides used the same wrong number.
- **F-011** — `tail_retention` reported *perfect* retention for a maximally degraded model.
  Nothing in the suite caught it: the toy path only ever exercised the working orientation.
- **F-014** — generation prompts were drawn from the training corpus, violating PROTOCOL §3
  partition disjointness. It surfaced as a crash only by luck.
- **F-015** — realised human spend differed 24% across arms, confounding the primary
  contrast. Two causes: the corpus ledger summed words while the policy priced in BPE, and
  terminal reconciliation fired one generation after the last one whose allocation is
  actually consumed. Now closed: spread is **0.04%**.
