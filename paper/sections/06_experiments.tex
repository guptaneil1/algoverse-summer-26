\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Experimental unit and estimand}

The experimental unit is one independently seeded recursive chain run to the frozen
horizon. Generations within a chain are repeated observations of the same unit, not
independent samples, so all uncertainty is computed across chains. The primary estimand is
the paired chain-level difference between the joint policy and the strongest eligible
non-joint baseline, where the baseline is fixed by the preregistered eligibility rule and
may not be chosen using primary treatment outcomes.

The primary outcome is the area under the generation-wise held-out human NLL-regret curve.
The confirmatory outcome is a frozen tail-retention measure evaluated on a partition never
used for selection. Chains are paired across policies by seed, over the ordered primary
seed set $101, 202, 303, 404, 505$.

\subsection{Fairness constraint}

Every policy in a budget-matched comparison consumes an identical total optimizer-token
count, which the corpus-assembly rule enforces by construction, and a lifetime count of
human-origin optimizer tokens matched as a common ceiling reached up to indivisibility
rather than as exact equality. Candidates are indivisible, so a policy cannot spend a
fraction of one; the realised spread is reported in Section~\ref{sec:results} and is an
order of magnitude below the practical effect threshold. A comparison is
invalid, and is reported as such rather than repaired, when it has mismatched paired seed
sets, unequal lifetime human-token budgets, unequal total optimizer-token budgets,
nonfinite policy scores, data leakage, or config and manifest hash mismatches. Poor or
harmful measured performance is not grounds for exclusion.

Token counts are taken from tokenized batches actually consumed by the optimizer, never
from character or document estimates, and padding is treated consistently and documented.

The executed configuration, the data-separation regime, and the positive-control reproduction are specified in Appendices~\ref{app:config},~\ref{app:separation} and~\ref{app:positive-control}.
